The Value of Making Things Clear

Sample essay · Design and communication

Clarity is a form of care. It asks us to do the work of understanding something before asking someone else to understand it.

Read essay

Before choosing a layout, an explanation, or a feature, ask what someone actually needs to know. A clear question makes it easier to decide what belongs and what can be removed.

A useful explanation gives each idea enough space. It provides context before detail and examples before abstractions. The goal is to help a reader build understanding one step at a time.

Simple does not have to mean shallow. A short overview can invite deeper exploration, as long as the path is clear.

Start Small, Stay Curious

Sample note · Learning and experimentation

A small experiment is a way to ask a question with something more concrete than a plan.

Read note

Pick the assumption that matters most. Build just enough to learn whether it holds. A narrow experiment gives you a result you can interpret, even if the result is that you were wrong.

Write down what you expected, what happened, and what you would try next. Over time, this record becomes a map of your learning.

The most interesting result is sometimes the one you did not expect. Leave enough room to notice it.
